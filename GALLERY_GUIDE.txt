GALLERY: ADDING PHOTOS AND STORIES

Gallery now has its own page at gallery.html. Header and footer links open this page.

1. Put a photo in assets, using a simple filename such as first-lab-meeting.jpg.
2. Open gallery.html. Remove the <div class="gallery-empty"> block when adding your first story.
3. Replace <div class="gallery-grid"></div> with this template, adapting the filename, dimensions, alt text, date, title, and story:

<div class="gallery-grid">
  <figure class="gallery-figure">
    <button class="gallery-photo-button" type="button" aria-label="Enlarge photo: our first lab meeting">
      <img src="assets/first-lab-meeting.jpg" alt="Kayode Lab members gathered for their first lab meeting" width="1200" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
    </button>
    <figcaption class="gallery-story">
      <time class="gallery-date" datetime="2026-10">October 2026</time>
      <h2 data-photo-caption>Our first lab meeting</h2>
      <p>Add a short story about the people, activity, or milestone pictured.</p>
    </figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

For more photos, add another <figure> inside the same gallery-grid. Keep newest entries first.
Use actual image dimensions for width and height. Thumbnail crops keep the grid tidy; clicking reveals the full photograph. Escape, Close, or clicking outside closes the enlarged view.

Upload the changed gallery.html and the new photos to GitHub together. index.html only needs updating when homepage content changes.
