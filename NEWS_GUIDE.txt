ADDING NEWS

Edit news-data.js to add or change an update. Both the homepage and news.html
read this same file. The homepage automatically shows only the four most recent
updates. Older news opens news.html, which shows every update, newest first.
You currently have two updates; no additional news has been invented.

Each entry has four fields:
  date: month in YYYY-MM format (for example 2026-10)
  category: a short label such as Lab milestone or Lab members
  title: the news headline
  body: the paragraph of news

Copy an existing entry and replace its values. Put a comma between entries.
Keep quotation marks around each field and value. If your text needs a double
quotation mark, write it as \" inside the value. Apostrophes need no change.
Use YYYY-MM dates only. Entries with the same date retain their order in the file.

Refresh your localhost page to review the result.
For later news updates, upload the edited news-data.js to GitHub.
