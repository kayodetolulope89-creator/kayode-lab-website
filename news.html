Kayode Lab welcomes new student researchers

We are pleased to welcome Samira Farooque, Javier Cuahtli Molina, and Bailey Brock to Kayode Lab at James Madison University. Their arrival marks an exciting step as we build our research team. We look forward to supporting their growth in molecular biology, diagnostics, and genomics through training, mentorship, and research.

August 2026Lab milestone

Kayode Lab begins at James Madison University

Dr. Tolulope A. Kayode has launched Kayode Lab in JMU’s Department of Biology. The lab brings together epidemiology, diagnostics, and genomics to study vector-borne disease transmission.
